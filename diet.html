Diet Plan

Weight aur muscle gain ke liye ghar ka Indian khana. Apna weight daal, target nikal, aur din bhar ka plan follow kar.

Tera roz ka target

Calories–kcal roz
Protein–gram roz
Paani–litre roz

Ye andaza hai: hafte mein 6 din gym + roz ka normal kaam, aur upar se lagbhag 400 kcal extra, taaki weight badhe. 2 hafte follow kar. Agar weight nahi badha to roz 200-300 kcal aur badha de.

Din bhar ka khana

Gym shaam ko maan ke time likha hai. Subah gym jaata hai to "Gym se pehle" wala snack gym se 1 ghanta pehle aur shake gym ke baad le. Calories aur protein lagbhag hain, ghar ke khaane mein thoda upar-neeche hota hai.

Calories badhani hain to ye jod

Zaroori baatein

Kya kha

  • Har meal mein protein: dal, paneer, dahi, doodh, anda, chicken, soya, chana.
  • Roti, rice, poha, oats: ye energy dete hain, kam mat kar.
  • Mungfali, badam, peanut butter, ghee: kam jagah mein zyada calories.
  • Roz 2 fruit (kela sabse sasta aur best).

Kya kam kar

  • Cold drink, packet wale chips, biscuit: calories hain par kaam ki nahi.
  • Roz bahar ka tala hua khaana.
  • Meal skip karna. Bhookh na ho tab bhi shake pi le.
  • Raat ko der tak jaagna. Muscle neend mein banti hai.

Budget tips

  • Soya chunks: 50 g mein 26 g protein, sabse sasta protein.
  • Bhuna chana + mungfali: sasta aur achha snack.
  • Whey protein zaroori nahi. Khaane se protein pura ho raha hai to theek hai.
  • Paneer mehnga lage to ghar pe doodh phaad ke bana le.

Weight diary

Hafte mein ek baar, subah uthke, toilet ke baad, khaali pet weight le aur yahan likh. Target: mahine mein 1-2 kg badhna.
