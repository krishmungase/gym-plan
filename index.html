Society Gym Plan

Weight aur muscle gain ke liye 6 din ka plan, Ravivar ko chhutti. Din chun, figure dekh, set karte jaa aur tick karta jaa.

Push: Chest, Shoulder, Tricep Pull: Back, Bicep Legs + Abs

Weight kab badhana

  • Pehle 2 hafte halka weight, sirf sahi tarika.
  • Saare sets mein upar wale reps (jaise 3 × 12) aaram se ho gaye, to agli baar thoda weight badha.
  • Har set ke last 1-2 reps mushkil lagne chahiye, form kharab nahi.

Khaana

  • Roz jitna khaata hai usse zyada kha.
  • Har meal mein protein: anda, paneer, dahi, doodh, chicken, dal, soya.
  • Protein: body weight (kg) × 1.6 gram roz.
  • Shake: 400 ml doodh + 2 kele + 2 chammach peanut butter + oats.

Aaram

  • 7-8 ghante neend, roz.
  • Joint mein tez dard ho to wo exercise turant rok.
  • Body bahut thaki ho to ek din chhutti le le.
  • Target: mahine mein 1-2 kg weight badhna.
